THE BIG PICTURE

Staying rooted shouldn’t be out of reach.

When housing costs rise faster than household incomes, families face difficult choices: spend less on essentials, move farther from work, or leave their neighborhood entirely.

This concern brings together the experiences of renters, homeowners, and people seeking stable housing. The shared goal is a community where more people can find a safe, affordable place to live.

What would progress look like?

  • More affordable homes near jobs, schools, and public transportation.
  • Greater housing stability for residents at risk of displacement.
  • Clear, public reporting on housing commitments and delivered results.

Who can act?

Responsibility is shared across public bodies. Each action should be linked to the body with authority to deliver it.
